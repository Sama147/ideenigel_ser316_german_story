One morning, a sleepy Hund walked into our classroom and stole the teacher's chair.
Meanwhile, the entire classroom was distracted by an uncontrollable Ohrwurm playing over the radio.
Rammstein was playing a sick guitar solo that had everyone headbanging.
Even the teacher joined in, waving a pair of Handschuhe like glow sticks while the Hund kept the beat.
The teacher offered the dog a Broetchen, hoping it would finally give back her chair.
The classroom Klimaanlage suddenly gave out a loud bang, filling the room with smoke, and the Hund promptly
dropped the Broetchen to chew on the Handschuhe instead. As the smoke cleared, the teacher discovered that Hansel
 and Gretel had escaped through the window and were driving away in the headmaster's Porsche.
The teacher sighed, grabbed a Staubsauger to clean up the mess, and muttered that this was the most
 wettbewerbsfaehig school in town. The hundemuede headmaster sighed, grabbed a stale Doener and an Apfelkuchen
 from the Baeckerei, and drove off down the Hauptstrasse without looking back.
 Just as the teacher finished cleaning, a Porsche crashed through the classroom wall, and the driver offered everyone fresh Doener.
