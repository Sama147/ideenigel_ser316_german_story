One morning, a sleepy Hund walked into our classroom and stole the teacher's chair.
Meanwhile, the entire classroom was distracted by an uncontrollable Ohrwurm playing over the radio.
Rammstein was playing a sick guitar solo that had everyone headbanging.
Even the teacher joined in, waving a pair of Handschuhe like glow sticks while the Hund kept the beat.
The teacher offered the dog a Broetchen, hoping it would finally give back her chair.
The classroom Klimaanlage suddenly gave out a loud bang, filling the room with smoke, and the Hund promptly dropped the Broetchen to chew on the Handschuhe instead.
As the smoke cleared, the teacher discovered that Hansel and Gretel had escaped through the window and were driving away in the headmaster's Porsche.
The teacher sighed, grabbed a Staubsauger to clean up the mess, and muttered that this was the most wettbewerbsfaehig school in town.
The hundemuede headmaster sighed, grabbed a stale Doener and an Apfelkuchen from the Baeckerei, and drove off down the Hauptstrasse without looking back.
Realizing his car was gone, the headmaster hopped onto an old Drahtesel parked outside, pedaling furiously while trying to balance a jar of Sauerkraut in his handlebars.
The teacher also tried to chase after them, but tripped on the cord for the staubsauger near her desk.
With a very disgruntled sigh, the teacher made a vow to visit the Biergarten late that day.
The hund, still lurking nearby and suddenly covercome with guilt, gallantly rushed to the teacher's rescue, only to charge headfirst into the desk at full speed, sending everything crashing down in a spectacular display of completely unhelpful heroism.
He suddenly lost control of the handlebars and swerved straight into a crowded Biergarten, crashing headfirst into a bewildered Pantoffelheld dressed in a festive Dirndel.
The Pantoffelheld hit the Hauptstrasse head first and began to have a dream about Saurkraut and it's delicious flavors before getting up and rushing off to find his wife. 
When he finally found her, she sighed and said, “The Sauerkraut is all gone, but at least there's some fresh Gebaeck!”
